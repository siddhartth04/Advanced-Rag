# Axonify HR Policies

## Time Off — by Region

### Canada (Waterloo + Toronto)
- **PTO (Paid Time Off):** 15 days accrued from day 1 (prorated for mid-year hires)
  - Carry-over: max 5 days into next year; remainder forfeited
- **Sick Days:** 10 days/year (no doctor's note required for <3 consecutive days)
- **Personal Days:** 6 days/year (can be used for any reason without explanation)
- **Statutory Holidays:** Ontario calendar (9 holidays: New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Civic Holiday, Labour Day, National Day for Truth & Reconciliation, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Boxing Day)
- **Total:** 41 days off/year

### US (Austin)
- **PTO:** Unlimited, with 10-day minimum use requirement/year
  - Policy: managers approve plans; Finance tracks actuals to prevent zero-use outliers
  - Rollover: unused PTO doesn't roll over (use-it-or-lose-it)
- **Sick Leave:** 10 days separate from PTO
- **Statutory Holidays:** US federal + Texas state (9 holidays)
- **Philosophy:** Trust-based; flexibility for high performers

### UK (London)
- **PTO:** 25 days + all UK public bank holidays (8: New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Early May Bank Holiday, Spring Bank Holiday, Summer Bank Holiday, Christmas, Boxing Day)
- **Sick Leave:** statutory SSP (Statutory Sick Pay, typically 80% of salary) topped up by Axonify to full salary for up to 20 days/year
- **Total:** 33 days off/year
- **No carry-over cap** (compliant with UK Working Time Regulations)

## Parental Leave

**Primary Caregiver (birth parent or designated):**
- 18 weeks at 100% salary
- Global (all regions)
- On top of any statutory entitlements

**Secondary Caregiver (non-birth parent):**
- 8 weeks at 100% salary
- Global

**Adoption:**
- Same policy as birth (18 weeks primary, 8 weeks secondary)

**Miscarriage / Pregnancy Loss:**
- 2 weeks leave, no documentation required

**Return-to-Work Options:**
- Full-time return (default)
- Phased return: 50% capacity for 4 weeks (optional)
- Extended timeline: negotiate with manager

**Job Protection:** Position held; transfer to similar role if position unavailable

## Remote Work Policy

**Default:** Hybrid (2 days in-office/week)

**Fully Remote:** Permitted with VP-level approval
- Preference: same timezone band as team

**Home Office Setup:**
- One-time stipend: $1,000 for new hires

## Performance Review Cycle

**Frequency:** Bi-annual (June mid-year, December annual)

**Process:**
1. Self-assessment: employee reflects on accomplishments, growth areas, OKRs
2. Manager assessment: manager evaluates performance against role expectations and OKRs
3. Calibration session: managers + VP discuss ratings across team
4. Feedback meeting: manager shares rating, feedback, development plan

**Rating Scale:**
- **Exceeds Expectations:** top 15% of performers (exceeds all core competencies, delivers beyond role)
- **Meets Expectations:** 70% of performers (meets role requirements, delivers on commitments)
- **Developing:** 10% of performers (needs improvement in 1–2 areas)
- **Below Expectations:** 5% of performers (not meeting core role requirements; PIP recommended)

**No forced curve:** Ratings based on actual performance, not quota

**OKRs:** Set at company level (CEO) → department → individual at start of each half (Jan, July)

**Promotions:**
- Decided at December review; effective February 1
- Mid-cycle promotions: for exceptional cases (rare)

## Compensation Philosophy

**Market Position:** Target 65th percentile of market
- Benchmark: Radford survey data (refreshed annually)
- Review: January; adjustments effective March 1

**Merit Increases (Annual):**
- Average: 4% in 2024 (range 0–10% based on rating)
- Exceeds: 8–10%
- Meets: 3–5%
- Developing: 1–2%
- Below: 0% (or separation)

**Equity (RSUs - Restricted Stock Units):**
- Vesting: 4 years with 1-year cliff
- Grant size: based on level and role (entry-level: 200k; senior: 500k+; executives: negotiated)
- Refresher grants: at year 3 for Meets+ performers (25% of original grant)

## Employee Conduct & Policies

**Code of Conduct:** All employees required to complete annually
- Anti-harassment, anti-discrimination, respectful workplace
- Confidentiality and IP assignment
- Conflict of interest disclosure

**Conflict Resolution:**
1. Direct conversation with colleague/manager
2. HR mediation
3. Formal grievance (documented, investigated)

**Termination:**
- At-will employment (US); equivalent in Canada/UK
- Severance: 1 week/year of service (minimum 4 weeks)
- Benefits: COBRA (US) or equivalent continuation
