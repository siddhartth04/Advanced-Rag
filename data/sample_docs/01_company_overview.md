# Axonify Corp — Company Overview

## Mission & Tagline

**Mission:** Axonify helps frontline workforces Learn → Retain → Perform through AI-powered microlearning that adapts to every individual.

We are on a mission to make learning a daily habit for the 2.7 billion deskless workers worldwide. These frontline employees — from retail associates to logistics coordinators to healthcare technicians — are the backbone of global commerce, yet they receive fragmented, outdated, and ineffective training. Traditional learning management systems (LMS) are massive, expensive, and rarely opened. We built Axonify on a single hypothesis: three-minute daily sessions beat three-hour annual compliance courses.

## Founding Story

Priya Mehta and James Okonkwo met in the University of Waterloo's AI lab in 2017. Priya had been researching spaced-repetition cognition science - the psychological principle that optimally-timed review maximizes long-term retention. James was building distributed systems and had just published research on fault-tolerant scheduling algorithms.

They saw the gap: enterprise L&D teams knew that annual e-learning compliance courses had near-zero long-term retention, yet they had no better alternative. Priya proposed: what if we use spaced repetition science + mobile-first design + gamification to make learning addictive and effective?

In 2019, they launched Axonify with $600,000 in seed funding from Waterloo angels and Canada's BDC. The first three customers were a retail chain (1,200 learners) and a logistics operator (800 learners). Within 18 months, Axonify had proven the model: learners who completed daily microlearning sessions showed 40% higher knowledge retention and 18% higher job performance scores than control groups.

## Market Position & Competitive Advantage

Axonify competes in the $8B global LMS market (Gartner 2024) against incumbents like Cornerstone OnDemand ($4B public company), SAP SuccessFactors, and Workday Learning. Axonify's differentiation:

1. **Mobile-first design** — 78% of Axonify learners access via mobile app vs. 30% for competitors
2. **Spaced repetition science** — Uses modified Ebbinghaus forgetting curve with 14 learner-specific decay parameters; competitors use simpler scheduling
3. **3-minute daily format** — Industry habit change: shift from annual 3-hour courses to daily 3-minute sessions
4. **Vertical specialization** — Built deep integrations with retail, logistics, healthcare, and financial services workflows
5. **AI-powered analytics** — Axonify Insights uses ML-based knowledge gap prediction (14 days ahead) vs. reactive reporting in competitors
