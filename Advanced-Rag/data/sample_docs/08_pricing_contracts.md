# Axonify Pricing & Commercial Terms

## Three Pricing Tiers

### Starter — $8/learner/month
**Minimum:** 500 learners, billed annually

**Includes:**
- Axonify Learn only (core product)
- Up to 10 active content modules
- Standard analytics (pre-built reports only, no custom reports)
- Email support (48-hour response SLA)
- SSO: SAML 2.0
- 99.5% uptime SLA
- US or Canada data residency
- Standard SCORM import
- Axonify mobile app (shared branded)

**Use case:** SMB retailers, small logistics operators with <1,000 employees

---

### Business — $12/learner/month
**Minimum:** 1,000 learners, billed annually

**Includes everything in Starter, plus:**
- Axonify Insights (full analytics: knowledge gap heatmaps, dashboards, custom reports)
- Unlimited content modules
- REST API access (1,000 req/min rate limit)
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager (monthly business reviews)
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Choice of US/EU/Canada data residency
- HRIS sync: BambooHR, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors (bi-directional)
- Slack + Microsoft Teams integrations
- Master Service Agreement (MSA) + Data Processing Agreement (DPA) required

**Use case:** Mid-market enterprises (1,000–10,000 employees)

---

### Enterprise — Custom pricing
**Minimum:** 5,000 learners, multi-year preferred

**Includes everything in Business, plus:**
- Axonify Connect (communications: push notifications, newsletters, surveys, emergency broadcast)
- Custom integrations (professional services hours included)
- 99.95% uptime SLA with financial penalties for breach
- Dedicated infrastructure option (single-tenant VPC)
- On-premise deployment option (additional licensing fee)
- FedRAMP Moderate (when authorized, Q3 2025 target)
- White-label mobile app (custom bundle ID, App Store presence)
- 24/7 phone support
- Executive business reviews (quarterly)
- Emergency broadcast feature

**Use case:** Large enterprises (>10,000 employees), regulated industries

---

## Contract Terms

**Annual Billing:** 100% upfront payment required

**Multi-Year Discount:** 5% discount per additional year (max 3-year term)
- Example: 2,000 learners on Business tier
  - Year 1: $12 × 2,000 × 12 = $288,000
  - 3-year: ($12 × 2,000 × 12) × 3 × 0.90 = $777,600 total (effective $259,200/year)

**Seat Overage:** $0.50/learner/month for seats above contracted volume, billed monthly in arrears

**Seat Reduction:** Permitted at renewal only, not mid-contract

---

## Refund Policy

**Starter Tier:**
- 30-day money-back guarantee from contract start date
- No questions asked, full refund

**Business & Enterprise Tiers:**
- No refund after 60 days from contract start
- Pro-rated refund within days 31–60
- Full refund only if Axonify commits material breach (uncured within 30 days)

---

## Data Export on Termination

**30-day export window** from contract end date
- CSV: learner data, completion records, knowledge scores
- xAPI statements: full event log in xAPI format for submission to external LRS
- SFTP or secure download link provided

**60-day deletion:** All data deleted from production and backup systems after 60 days per security policy

---

## SLA Credits

**Uptime below SLA triggers monthly service credits (not cash refunds):**
- 99.0–99.5%: 10% monthly credit
- 98.0–99.0%: 25% monthly credit
- Below 98.0%: 50% monthly credit

Credits applied to next invoice. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of incident via support portal.

**Uptime calculation:** Based on successful HTTP responses from the `/health` endpoint measured from 3 global regions.

## Customer Success Metrics & At-Risk Playbook

**Health Metrics Tracked Per Customer:**
- DAU/MAU ratio (target >40%; below 25% triggers at-risk flag)
- Average session completion rate (target >85%)
- Knowledge retention improvement at 30/60/90 days (target +15% vs baseline)
- Streak maintenance rate (% learners with 5+ day streak in past 30 days)

**At-Risk Triggers:**
- DAU/MAU <25% for 2 consecutive weeks
- NPS <40 in survey
- Renewal risk flagged by CSM

**Escalation Playbook:**
1. CSM escalation call within 48 hours
2. Executive sponsor email from VP Sales
3. Insights deep-dive to identify engagement drop cause
4. Joint action plan with 30-day check-in
5. If unresolved in 60 days: escalate to VP Customer Success

---

## Expansion & Upsell

**Common Expansion Paths:**
1. Seat growth (most common): +20% seats year-over-year
2. Tier upgrade: Starter → Business → Enterprise
3. Module expansion: additional SCORM packages, specialized content
4. Language expansion: add new languages (customers in APAC often add Japanese, Mandarin)
5. Regional expansion: add new countries/locations (rollout to 2+ regions in year 1/2)

**Success team targets:** 120% NRR (expansion outpacing churn)
